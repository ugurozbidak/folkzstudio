Your people, wherever you land.

What’s inside

City Chat

Talk with members who are in the same city as you right now, whether you’re based there or passing through.

Map and feed

Posts and local knowledge from the city you’re in: where to eat, what to see, what to skip.

Discover your HUB

Find pilots, cabin crew and ground staff from your base and across the network, and connect through message requests.

Visibility you control

Choose whether members at your own HUB can see you. Change it any time from your profile.

Private by design

Folkz is invite-only and built for aviation professionals. Your device location is only used to set your city. It is never stored on our servers, never shared with other members and never tracked in the background.

Folkz is a social space, not an operational one. Flight numbers, crew lists, rosters and hotel details are never shared here.
