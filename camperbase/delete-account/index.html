CamperBase Support Privacy

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your CamperBase account and its data at any time from the app.

Delete in the app

Open CamperBase, go to Settings and choose to delete your account. Your personal data is removed within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it. Anonymized usage data may be kept for statistics.

Can’t open the app?

Email info@camperbase.org from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my CamperBase account”. We’ll confirm once it’s done.

Your subscription

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel your subscription.

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions, otherwise it will keep renewing.

Need a hand?

Questions about CamperBase, your membership or your data. Write to us and we’ll get back to you.
