Most answers are below. If yours isn’t, write to info@camperbase.org and we’ll get back to you.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose CamperBase. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions. You can change or cancel there.

Refunds follow Apple’s or Google’s store policies. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why does CamperBase ask for my location?

With your permission, location is used to show nearby camping spots on the map, surface events and announcements for your area and track an active trip. You can change this any time in your device settings.

How do I report a post, review or user?

Email info@camperbase.org with a short description and, if you can, a screenshot. We review every report.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account and data from the app settings. Full steps are on the account deletion page.

How do I access, correct or delete my data?

Email info@camperbase.org. Read more in the Privacy Policy.
